Six habits that reduce phishing risk.

  1. Check the domain name carefully.
  2. Do not blindly click suspicious links.
  3. Verify through an official source.
  4. Be cautious with urgent messages.
  5. Watch for spelling or domain changes.
  6. Never enter sensitive data on an untrusted page.
